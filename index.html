IPConfig gives creators a timestamped, tamper-evident evidence record of their intellectual property claims — with onchain licensing, revenue tracking, and dispute management.

⚖️

Important legal notice: Registering a work on IPConfig creates a timestamped, verifiable evidence record — it does not grant legal copyright protection and does not constitute legal proof of authorship or originality. A cryptographic hash demonstrates that a specific file existed at the time of registration, not that the registrant created it. Legal copyright recognition is determined by applicable national law, not by blockchain registration. Consult a legal professional for formal intellectual property protection.

🔏

Tamper-evident records

Versioned license terms

Registration on IPConfig records your claim and work hash on a blockchain. This provides evidence that a specific file existed at this time — it does not automatically prove authorship, originality, or grant legal copyright. The hash alone does not prove you created the work.
Work details

Work hash — evidence of existence
The file is hashed locally using SHA-256. Only the hash is submitted to the blockchain — never the file itself.
Rights holder
The declared holder of rights over this IP. Can differ from registrant.
What gets recorded

Recorded on-chain:

  • Title, category, description
  • Declared creation date
  • Block timestamp of registration
  • SHA-256 hash of your work
  • Rights holder wallet address
  • Metadata URI (if provided)

Not recorded:

  • The actual file or its contents
  • Personal identifying information
  • Legal evidence beyond the timestamp
